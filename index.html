Sources & data

All figures include their year, age group where applicable, and geographic scope. The international comparison covers jobs, poverty, education, depression, digital life, migration and opportunities. Family and peer pressure are compared through fictional situations, not national rankings. Poverty figures are historical (2013/2018), and missing values are not zero. Other comparison years are shown on the graphs. Youth unemployment uses 2024 ILO modelled estimates (World Bank). The Morocco-only slide uses the HCP national survey, which reports a different rate. Internet use is for the whole population, not only youth. Other measures use the indicated report or survey years. Regional survey results are not world averages.
